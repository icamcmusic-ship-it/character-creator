### 1b. Bugs in generated text

These are visible on almost every sheet.

- **T1: "they goes still" / "they meets it head-on".** The phrases in `STRATEGY_BY_STRESS` (`engine.js:5893-5898`) are third-person singular, but they are spliced in after "they":
  - `motivationChain` (the method link, about line 5930)
  - `backstoryBeats` (the failure beat, line 7099)
  - `foilPremiseFromSheets` (`mechanics.js:386-393`)

  It appeared in every sheet that had a stress card. Store the phrases as bare verbs and conjugate them where they are used.
- **T2: Trait names pasted in as clauses.** Examples:
  - "…was when **keeps a spreadsheet for everything** made them useful — **a very small life, kept perfect** set, from then on…"
  - "Before anything else there was **was**…" (11 in 200)
  - Hyphenated names left raw, as in "They want **a-quiet-partnership**".

  Add one `asClause(t)` / `asNounPhrase(t)` normaliser and send every splice through it: `_mxQ`/`_mxLc` in `mechanics.js:29-30`, and the raw `.toLowerCase()` calls in `engine.js:7087-7102`.
- **T3: Voice-lab transforms.**
  - The echo transform (`engine.js:7281`) repeats whatever the last word is: "I'm not going to answer that, **that**."
  - `{task}` can produce "I'm not going to **wait with me**".
  - The afterthought is attached with no check on meaning.
- **T4: Contradictions compare unrelated things.** `contradictionFor` (`engine.js:3583`) pairs *any* two cards that share an axis. For example, "Parents who liked each other" (Positive Origins) is contrasted with a mannerism. Restrict it to comparable sections.
  - The "when" field is empty about 80% of the time. Fall back to `derivedContextTags` (`mechanics.js:188`), which exists but isn't used here.
- **T5: Arc shifts collapse into bland traits.** `_mxShift` (`mechanics.js:325-331`) picks *any* trait at intensity ±1. The result is text like "They choose an ordinary week over **mild preference**". Keep the concept family when shifting.

## 2. QOL, UI and UX

The page is about 26,600px tall on desktop and 34,500px on mobile, with about 635 buttons rendered up front. Generation is fast, under 1.2s.

### P0

1. **Scroll to the result after a build.** Ctrl+Enter and Build leave the viewport on the controls. The sheet is thousands of pixels below, which is worst on mobile. After a build, scroll `#sheetTitle` into view, or show a "Jump to sheet ↓" toast.
2. **Onboarding is buried.** `#onboard` (`index.html:598`) sits inside `#emptyState`, after all the controls, so a first-time user never sees it without scrolling. Move it directly under the tabs.
3. **Too many "roll" buttons compete:** Build & Roll, Roll 5 pick one, 🎲 Randomize All, Surprise me and Roll everything (`index.html:325, 493-495, 621, 862`). Keep one primary button and put the rest in a "More ways to roll ▾" menu. Randomize All and Roll everything are near-duplicates, so drop one.
4. **Fix the dark sticky bar** (B26).

### P1: information overload and discoverability

5. **The controls are one long column of `<details>`** (`index.html:151-480`): Constraints, Budgets, Why-not, Seed, Profile, Packs, 13 axes, Affinity and Custom archetype.
   - Group them into Shape, Rules, Library and Diagnostics.
   - When a closed panel has active settings, show a count, e.g. "Constraints · 3 active".
   - Add a "Settings are affecting this roll" chip next to Build. Today, hidden constraints can skew output with no visible sign.
6. **Sheet-level features are hard to find.** Voice lab, Arc, Pressure, Project library and "Why does this feel familiar?" all sit below the sheet. Turn the sticky bar's "Jump to…" select into a real sub-nav (Sheet | Voice | Arc | Pressure | Project). Add a one-time hint on the first card: "📌 keep the intent, 🔒 freeze it".
7. **The Project library is the main place work is saved, but it is a folded panel at the bottom.** Put "Project: Untitled ▾" and an autosave indicator in the header.
8. **The action row under the sheet is crowded** (`index.html:517-555`). Split it into Edit (undo, redo), Keep (save, add to cast, file into project) and Share (copy, file menu, print). Move **Reset to Defaults** away from Save, and make it undoable.
9. **Jargon needs plain-language hints** that also work on tap (a `title` alone does nothing on mobile): lenses, divergence, affinity weights, foil, diversity objective, pressure, edges, and "Prefer ×3 / Rarely ×¼".
   - Lens chips need a visible selected state, `aria-pressed` and a Clear button.
   - Use one verb (Build or Roll) consistently.
   - Label the 🛠 badge "Tinker mode".
10. **Relationships tab empty state.** Say "Needs at least 2 cast members" and add a button that goes to Cast.

### P1: mobile (390px)

11. The sticky bar scrolls sideways with no visual cue, and Undo gets clipped. Collapse it to Build, Undo and a ⋯ menu, or add an edge fade.
12. After the first build, collapse the inputs into an "Edit inputs ▾" summary with a one-line recap.
13. The tabs stack as three full-width pills. Use a segmented control with short labels (Single / Cast / Relations).
14. The header wraps onto three lines. Shrink the h1 and subtitle below about 420px.

### P2

15. **Keyboard.** Add a real shortcut cheat-sheet dialog; today `?` only opens a `<details>`. Add these keys:
    - `R`: reroll unpinned
    - `L` / `P`: lock or pin the focused card
    - `1`–`3`: switch tabs
    - `/`: search
    - `Ctrl+S`: save
    - `Esc`: close the File menu
16. **The File menu has 9 formats in one list.** Split it into **Share** (link, LLM prompt, markdown) and **Backup** (character, cast and workspace JSON, full backup), with a one-line description under each item.
17. **Toasts.** Add action buttons (Undo, View sheet) and queue toasts rather than overwrite them. Announce "Built: 42 traits, 3 kept" in the `role=status` region.
18. **Seed chip.** Make the sticky seed chip click-to-copy, with a tooltip that explains what a seed promises. After B3 is fixed, the explanation should be true.
19. **Undo coverage.** Extend undo to Reset, Unpin all, Unlock all, Clear constraints, Clear budgets, Delete archetype and project deletes. Use "Undo" toasts rather than `confirm()`.
20. **Theme toggle.** The CSS already supports `data-theme` (`style.css:130`), but there is no visible toggle. Add light / dark / system.

## 3. Small side features, robustness and complexity

### Side features

- **History drawer:** the last 20 rolls with restore, built on the existing undo stack.
- **Side-by-side diff** of two rolls, showing traits and sliders.
- **Favourite traits:** a personal "always consider" list with a soft ×2 weight.
- **Slider lock:** Randomize only touches the axes you haven't locked.
- **Named slider presets:** save and load slider sets quickly, without the full archetype builder.
- **Search the rendered sheet,** and remember which sections are collapsed.
- **Voice lab extras:** "Copy line" on each line, plus word count and reading level per line.
- **Cast: "Regenerate the most similar member"**, using `optimiseCastVoices`' own score.
- **More export formats:**
  - Rich-HTML clipboard, for pasting into Google Docs.
  - Cast as CSV.
  - Short and full LLM prompt variants.
  - Relationship web as SVG.
- **Density options:** a compact density mode and a reduce-motion option.

### Robustness

- **Storage failures.** Show a banner when storage is unavailable or full; the probe already exists (`app.js:30`). Add a "last backup N days ago" reminder.
- **Imports.** Check the schema version, list what was skipped, and offer merge or replace for casts and workspaces.
- **Conflicting constraints.** Check before generating: "always X" together with "never X's category", or a cap of 0 on a required tier. Run "Why didn't I get…" automatically when a roll fails, and name the rule that caused it.
- **Unsaved work.** Warn with `beforeunload` when work hasn't been filed into a project.
- **Old share links.** Links with an old version prefix should show a message, not a blank sheet.
- **Lazy rendering.** Render collapsed advanced panels only when opened, to improve first paint on mobile.
- **Test coverage.** Add tests for every bug above. None of them are caught by the current 216 tests. Most useful:
  - A browser test that clicks each chip × button and budget input.
  - A test that the printed seed replays after 5 rolls in a session.
  - An import → arc test.
  - A batch → arc test.
  - A regex over 200 sheets for `\bthey (goes|meets|runs|shuts)\b`, `there was was`, and hyphenated trait names inside prose.

## 4. Trait and archetype balance

### 4a. Trait bank

- **Category sizes vary widely:** 6 to 127 traits, median 54. **38 categories have fewer than 10 traits.** The smallest:

  | Category | Traits |
  |---|---|
  | Breath & Stamina | 6 |
  | Physical & Slapstick | 6 |
  | Doesn't Get Jokes | 6 |
  | Youth Register | 7 |
  | Era References | 7 |
  | Tech Vintage | 7 |
  | Scripted Speech | 7 |
  | Sensory Load | 7 |
  | Masking | 7 |
  | Gallows | 7 |
  | Teasing as Affection | 7 |
  | Laughs at Own Jokes | 7 |

  Set a floor of about 15 traits per category.
- **Rarity and intensity are tied together** (Cramér's V 0.527):
  - 2,523 of 2,977 *distinctive* traits are intensity 3.
  - *Signature* traits are almost all intensity 4–5.
  - Only 26 *common* traits are intensity 5.

  The cells that need filling are quiet signatures (subtle but rare), quiet distinctives, and loud commons. These are what make a character feel specific without feeling loud.
- **Axis polarity:**
  - Discipline is skewed positive (60.2%).
  - Volume, ego and mood lean negative (42–45%).
  - Add more low-discipline traits and more loud, confident and cheerful ones.
- **26.3% of traits (2,461) have no polarity.** The conflict check (`checkConflictsFor`, `engine.js:3421`) only works through opposing polarity, so these traits can never conflict with anything and the sliders can't reach them. The worst sections are Mannerisms (31% polarised), Dialogue Grammar (39%) and Vocabulary (41%). Either add polarity, or add an explicit table of conflicting pairs (for example "whispers" vs "booming").
- **26 near-duplicate pairs**, mostly an older trait next to a later pack's rewrite:
  - 100033 / 110085 ("anxious about small things" / "…trivia")
  - 1439 / 3902 "Overcommitting"
  - 2514 / 3726 "Correction-unafraid"
  - 2773 / 4196 "Escalation-comfortable"
  - 1288 / 3905 "Rule-bending"
  - 3194 / 5151 "No-hyperbole"
  - 1442 / 3926 "Martyr-willing"

  Merge them or make them clearly different. There are also 8 exact duplicate example lines.
- **80 trait names are over 60 characters.** They are sentences, not labels, and they break the splices in T2.
- **Review status:** 8,753 of 9,362 traits are still `unreviewed`.

### 4b. Archetypes (52 presets, `engine.js:847`)

- **The presets differ on paper.** No two are closer than cosine 0.75. The closest pairs are Burnt-Out Idealist / Grieving Parent, Wide-eyed Child / Cheerful Mess, and Quietly Furious Caretaker / Stubborn Craftsman.
- **The generated sheets are much more alike.** Measured as the average category cosine across 30 sheets per preset, all pairs average 0.70. The closest pairs:

  | Pair | Category cos | Trait cos |
  |---|---|---|
  | Charming Manipulator / Lovable Liar | 0.93 | 0.35 |
  | Reluctant Second-in-Command / Quietly Furious Caretaker | 0.89 | 0.31 |
  | Quietly Excellent Professional / Stubborn Craftsman | 0.89 | 0.28 |
  | Company Loyalist / Career Bureaucrat | 0.89 | 0.25 |
  | Workaholic Avoiding a Diagnosis / Steady Organiser | 0.88 | 0.30 |

- **Some presets barely differ from a blank roll** (category cosine to the blank baseline): Black-Sheep Returnee 0.885, Contented Elder 0.879, Workaholic Avoiding a Diagnosis 0.864, Diaspora Code-Switcher 0.861.
- **Profile hints are clustered.** Hints go mostly to attachment, stress, values and humor. No preset hints any category in Motivation, Goals, Texture, Repair, Contradiction, Role by Context, Romance, Dialect, Beliefs, Jargon, Conversation, Body, Money, Fears or Family. Eight of the 15 Humor categories are never hinted, and Diaspora Code-Switcher doesn't hint `dialect:Code-Switching`.
- **Least-used axes:** rebelliousness, intelligence, assertiveness and manners (25–27 presets each). Only 2 presets set verbosity to ±2.

**Recommendations**

1. **Give every preset 2–4 "signature" hints in the sections that are off by default.** These would switch on for that archetype only. Examples:
   - Diaspora Code-Switcher → Code-Switching
   - Scattered Genius → Info-Dumping
   - Contented Elder → Era References
   - Career Bureaucrat → Legal jargon
   - Grieving Parent → Family Talk
   - Workaholic Avoiding a Diagnosis → Body in Speech
2. **Separate the look-alike pairs** with contrasting humor, role and values hints:
   - Charming Manipulator: Cruel/Cutting humor, Instigator role.
   - Lovable Liar: Self-deprecating humor, Connector role.
3. **Push the flat presets further.** Use more ±2 axes and stronger `must` axes for the four near-blank presets.
4. **Add presets for under-served shapes:**
   - loud-and-kind
   - disciplined rebel
   - anxious leader
   - cheerful nihilist
   - low-intellect sage (street-wise)
   - polite liar

### 4c. Coverage in 300 default rolls

- **5,827 traits (62%) were never drawn.** The Gini coefficient of trait usage is 0.78.
- **690 traits are in the 12 sections that are off by default** (`engine.js:3988-4017`), so they have 0% reach. Consider turning Conversation Mechanics, Family Talk and Fears on by default, or letting each sheet enable one off-by-default section at random.
- **Verbosity reaches only 11% of its traits.** At level 0, only *Pacing & Situation-Driven* is drawn. That leaves four categories of about 125 traits each unused at default settings.
- **Reach by rarity:** common 50%, signature 22%. Raise the weights for distinctive and signature traits a little, or make it a slider.
- **Categories on every sheet:** 11 categories appeared in all 300 sheets (7 Motivation, 3 Goals, Verbosity Pacing). This is a fixed skeleton that makes sheets feel structurally the same; see §5.

## 5. Keeping output fresh: avoiding repeated character types

**What the numbers show:** the trait draw itself is varied. 200 characters gave 199 distinct category combinations. The feeling of sameness comes from the **fixed prose layers written on top of the draw**: chain, beats, pressure, recovery, voice tails and foils. These are fixed sentence frames keyed on 4–5 category buckets.

### 5a. Quick wins (repeated text, quoted from 200 sheets)

- **Voice tails.**
  - `VOICE_FRAGMENTS.tail.warm` (`engine.js:6590`) has one entry. Every warm character ends every line with "We're all right, though. You and me." (10 of 10 takes).
  - `PRESSURE_TAIL` has one line per stress type; Freeze is just "...".
  - The habitual device always takes the *first* vocabulary example ("Three are dead." in 4 of 10 takes).

  Use pools of 6–10 lines with no repeats within a set of takes, cycle through all the examples, and cap any one tail at about 1 take in 3.
- **Pressure and recovery.**
  - Six pressure lines open with the same prefix in 200 of 200 sheets ("The first tell:", "They reach for the defence:" …).
  - Recovery is a lookup over 4 stress × 4 attachment types: "They need to move — a walk…" ×71, "They go to nobody…" ×63.

  Give each row 4–6 phrasings chosen by a sheet hash. Build all **16 stress × attachment combinations**; a Fight + Anxious recovery is not a Fight + Avoidant one. Vary which stages exist; some characters go straight from irritated to broken.
- **Chain and beats.**
  - The fear link opens "The thing they organise their life to avoid is…" in 200 of 200 sheets.
  - "The latest time it broke:" appears ×104.
- **Emergent names.** They are always "The [Adj] [Noun]", with repeats such as "The Exiled Conscience" ×6 and "The Iron Standard" ×5. Add other shapes: "[Noun] Who [verb phrase]", "[Noun] of [thing]", and "The [X] in [Y]'s Coat" for blends.
- **Foil premises.** There are 6 fixed frames; "B is what A's fear looks like lived in…" came up 3 times in 6. Add frames driven by humor, attachment, competence and origin, and avoid reusing a frame for the same source character.

### 5b. Structural variety

- **Several chain structures,** chosen per sheet:
  - wound-led
  - need-led (they know what they need and refuse it)
  - inverted (the Lie was once true)
  - positive-origin-only (no wound)
- **Several beat structures:**
  - a loss that arrives late in life
  - two formative events that contradict each other
  - a false turning point
  - a chronic "nothing happened" wound
- **Vary the skeleton.** Let sheet shape drop or swap one of the 11 always-present categories (for example, no explicit Ghost; a Need expressed only through a Contradiction).
- **Voice leans blend.** Today the lean `order` (`engine.js:7242`) takes the first match, so every assertive character talks "direct". Pick two leans weighted by strength and blend them, and add pools for combined leans such as "the confident lie" (assertive + dishonest).

### 5c. Generation modes that break patterns

- **Archetype blending in Surprise me:** two presets with a slider split, and a name built from both.
- **Anti-archetypes:** invert a preset's axes but keep its motivation hints ("a Mentor who hoards knowledge").
- **Lens dice:** random pairs of lenses, including clashing ones such as Court + Adolescent.
- **An "against type" dial:** force one or two high-weight traits from the side of an axis opposite to the preset.
- **A rotating wild section:** each sheet turns on one section that is off by default, seeded.
- **An inner conflict engine** (the strongest single lever; §6).
- **A longer-term novelty memory.** The project archive already weights novelty. Extend it to prose templates and emergent names, not just categories, so the *text* doesn't repeat across a session.

## 6. Shallow or incomplete mechanics, and gaps in the trait bank

### 6a. Mechanics

| Mechanic | Where it is thin | How to deepen it |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice lab** (`voiceRules`, `engine.js:6512`) | Reads only verbosity, register, grammar, vocabulary and axes. It never reads motivation, humor or mannerisms. Every character shares one topic pool of 24/21/18 items. | • *Concealing* circles the Ghost or Wound; *lying* protects the Want; *asking for help* is bent by the Defence.<br>• Humor adds a transform: Dry → understatement, Self-deprecating → self-jab, Cruel → barb at the listener.<br>• Mannerisms add stage directions (*[looks at the door]*).<br>• Build per-character topic pools from Goals, Jargon and Family. |
| **Lenses** | Affect only the draw and the voice topics. `activeLens` is never read in `mechanics.js`.
