## 0. Summary and verdict

**State of the project.** The September audit's work holds up. `node tests/run.js` passes 307/307, and the browser suite passes 75/75 in both normal and strict-CSP mode. Seed replay matched in 150 of 150 explicit-seed and 150 of 150 printed-seed checks (v1 and v2), 24 of 24 share-link round trips matched, 400 random undo/redo operations restored the exact prior state, and three 1,500-action monkey runs hit one page error. The bank is 9,868 traits (the September audit counted 9,362).

**The problems have changed character.** September's bugs were wiring bugs that a click would have shown. This round's worst findings are silent: they do not throw, and the tests cannot see them.

**The ten findings that matter most**

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stored/imported XSS.** `render.js:1416` puts trait names into `#warnBox.innerHTML` unescaped. An imported character file whose trait ids aren't in the bank runs script on import (reproduced). No CSP, and the action dispatcher will call any global function name, so an injection has a ready gadget. | High |
| 2 | **24 duplicate keys in `WEIGHT_MATRIX`** (`engine.js:2228-2513`). The later key silently replaces the earlier, so about 39 designed cross-links never fire, including every stress → Recovery & Repair link. Measured effect is a 6.5-point average loss of enrichment, up to 20 points. | High |
| 3 | **598 trait examples carry literal `<i>(…)</i>`.** The renderer escapes them, so users read raw tags on cards, in the headline voice sample and in the voice lab. | High (visible) |
| 4 | **Dark mode is unfinished.** Card descriptions are 1.83:1 contrast (hard-coded light-theme colour), the sticky bar stays light, and white-on-pink and white-on-gold fail. | High (visible) |
| 5 | **The wildcard toggle and count slider do nothing** while Sheet shape is on, which is the default. Measured: byte-identical sheets with the toggle on and off. | Medium-High |
| 6 | **Undo, redo and history-restore leave the seed chip and share link on the roll you undid away from.** Copy link then opens a different character than the one on screen. | Medium-High |
| 7 | **Import and load paths commit before they validate.** Some malformed files show "Could not import" but have already replaced the sheet, and one poisons Undo. A saved record with no `state` bricks Build until reload. | Medium |
| 8 | **Three silent engine regressions:** Surprise me overwrites the preset's own hints (hint realisation 19% against 56%); cast optimisation re-creates duplicate stress + attachment pairs (0.017 → 0.333 per cast); Retire-for-project has no effect on any build. | Medium |
| 9 | **"They reads it as…" appears on 300 of 300 pressure sheets,** plus hyphen-slug trait names, doubled words and leaked category labels in prose. | Medium |
| 10 | **A `node_modules` symlink to `/opt/node22/lib/node_modules` is committed in HEAD.** `.gitignore` says `node_modules/`, which does not match a symlink. | Medium |

**Why output feels samey, in one paragraph.** Whole-sheet repetition is not the problem: 500 blank sheets share 0.56 of about 44 traits on average (max 6), and axis space shows no clusters. The staleness has three measured sources. First, the seven Motivation pools hold only 468 cards for 7 of every 44 slots, so 11% of sheet pairs share at least one. Second, the prose written on top of the draw reuses fixed frames: 9 prose layers are under 50% distinct, and some arc steps have 3 distinct strings in 360 samples. Third, 1,104 traits in 13 off-by-default sections cannot appear on a blank roll, and the voice-side pools (grammar, verbosity, vocabulary, mannerisms) reach only 35–42%. Section 5 ranks the fixes.

**What the tool does well** (so it isn't lost in the changes): no preset-pair is closer than category cosine 0.862, 13 personality sliders respond strongly, content packs and constraints survived fuzzing with zero exceptions, import handling of 8 bad file types is clean, the service worker and offline mode work, and the engine is deterministic.

## 5. Keeping output fresh

### 5a. Diagnosis (measured, 500 blank sheets and 300 sheets of prose)

**What is not the problem**
- Whole-sheet overlap is effectively nil: mean 0.56 shared traits of about 44, maximum 6; 1 pair in 124,750 shares 6 or more. Overlap with the previous five sheets is 0.0057.
- Axis space is a smooth cloud (k-means silhouette 0.07–0.08). Role, values, attachment, stress, vices, competence, origins and contradiction marginals are flat.
- Best-of-three exploration and the project archive do not buy novelty. Together they add about 1.5% trait diversity and 2% label variety; their real effect is coherence (12.4 against 5.6). The archive improves category evenness by under 1 point and slightly *reduces* label variety (180 → 168 per 250 sheets).

**What is the problem**
1. **Small motivation pools.** Want 77, Fear 73, Wound 68, Lie 69, Need 61, Ghost 60, Defence 60 (468 cards) fill 7 slots on every sheet. 10.97% of sheet pairs share at least one of the 7 cards, so someone generating 20 characters sees a repeat in about 1 pair in 9. "Being found ordinary" opens 5.0% of sheets. Eight of the 56 Need cards say "to be allowed to do it badly". Eight categories (Want, Fear, Wound, Lie, Need and three Goals cards) appear on **100%** of sheets, so every character has the same Want–Fear–Wound–Lie–Need stack and goal triple.
2. **A repeated motif:** dog-themed Ghost and Goal cards appear on more than 10% of blank sheets.
3. **Fixed prose frames.** Layers with low distinctness: arcChange 19%, arc 13%, edge 17%, castRecovery 23%, web 23%, castSeat 24%, recovery 35%, softTension 15%, emergentPattern 15%. Single skeletons dominate: `contradiction:who` has 4 distinct sentences in 296 ("<Attachment> attachment decides who gets which face", 31%); `innerConflict:question` 4 in 300 (37% one sentence); `seated:What is it for?` 8 in 439; `pressureChain:Where it tips` 5 in 300; `edge:obligation` 8 in 510, and its top answer is literally "None." (15.3%). **Four of the 16 arc steps have 3 distinct strings in 360 samples** (for example "Nothing yet, which is the problem." is 33% of every step-1 cost in the corruption arc).
4. **Chains look the same:** every chain paragraph is the same six-sentence skeleton with traits inserted, and the "alternate chain structures" the README claims show only as opener variation (68% "the belief was learned from").
5. **Reach.** 1,104 traits in 13 off-by-default sections are unreachable on blank rolls; the four voice-side pools (3,086 traits, 31% of the bank) reach only 35–42%, with 8.6 slots per sheet. Intensity 5 is drawn 3.5× less than its bank share (1.7% against 5.9%).
6. **The same quoted text appears four times per sheet.** The Lie or Want text is the content of the chain, arc, recovery, inner-conflict and voice lines, so the user perceives one idea restated. (Good for coherence, tiring for variety.)
7. **Labels repeat.** 306 distinct emergent labels in 500 sheets; the top ones appear 9–13 times ("The Investing Patron" ×13); about 40% of sheets share a label with another.
8. **The conflict checker has stopped discriminating:** blank sheets average 7.8 Strong and 0.23 Jarring flagged conflicts, and 98% have at least one, because Motivation and Goals cards are drawn independently of each other and of personality.
9. **Surprise me's "free" mode** is contradiction-saturated and its preset mode ignores the preset (M7).

### 6b. Trait database: gaps

Method: 270 behavioural concepts were probed by regex over name, description and example. **74 do not appear even in a trait name, and 16 have zero hits anywhere.** A hit can be incidental, so zero and near-zero rows are the reliable evidence. Full tables and **144 drafted candidate traits** (each checked against its two nearest existing traits; cosine 0.15–0.41) are in `detail-trait-bank.md` §7.

**Concepts with zero matches:** institutional or royal "we"; innuendo ("that's what she said"); love-bombing; moral licensing (does good to excuse bad); self-aggrandising mock-arrogance; white lies as social lubricant; demanding rather than asking; silence out of shame; a story that starts at the wrong end or spoils its ending; retelling others' stories as one's own; stories that always turn back to the teller; playing hard to get; grief humour about the deceased; gambling with others' money or feelings; the bossy-older-sibling parenting voice; therapy-averse "I don't do feelings talk".

## 8. How this was checked, and what was not

**Method.** Four parallel investigations over the code at `bc9be10`: a bug hunt (static lint, fuzzers over seeds, settings, imports, undo, packs; Playwright import, XSS and monkey runs), a browser UX audit (Chromium, five widths, both themes, scripted contrast, target-size, CLS and accessibility scans, print, offline), a trait-bank audit (inventory, grids, polarity, duplicates, text form, reach across 32,912 builds in 7 regimes, 270 concept probes), and a balance and mechanic-depth audit (58 presets × 40 sheets, 500 blank sheets, 39,460 prose items, settings-effect sweeps). I spot-checked the key claims against the code (H1, H3, H4, M1, M3, T1 and the dead matrix keys). Reproduction scripts and raw outputs are not committed because several are tens of megabytes; `dupkeys.js` is included.
