## 0. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (numbers detailed below)
- Whole-sheet repetition is NOT the problem: 500 blank sheets share on average 0.56 of ~44 traits (max 6), no real clusters in axis space (silhouette 0.07-0.08). Staleness comes from (a) small pools (Want/Fear/Wound/Lie/Need/Ghost/Defence = 468 cards; 11% of sheet pairs share one), (b) fixed prose frames (arc steps with 3 distinct strings in 360 samples; 9+ layers under 50% distinct), (c) a content reach gap (1,104 off-by-default traits 0% reachable on blank rolls; voice-side pools 35-42% reach, Gini 0.65).
- Presets: hints work on average (3.21x lift) but steer only default-on sections; 0/58 hint Motivation/Goals/Contradiction, 7 contextrole hints are exact no-ops, humor hints realise 36%; 65 of 312 axis-pair quadrants have no preset (all cold/low poles); 5 lookalike clusters; 7 of 12 candidate presets tested well.
- Real bugs found (all silent): WEIGHT_MATRIX 24 duplicate keys = 39 dead links (measured +6.5 points of lost enrichment, up to +20); wildcard toggle/count dead while Sheet shape is on; cast optimiser re-creates duplicate stress+attachment pairs (.017 -> .333); Surprise me overwrites preset hints (hint realisation 19% vs 56%) and sets divergence .35-.75; "They reads it as" in 298/300 sheets; "the the", leaked category labels, hyphen slugs.
- Mechanics are broad but shallow in 4 places: 7 voice situations only; lenses reach prose only for 4 of 15; arcs are 16 fixed steps; pair analysis ignores motivation.

### 4.5 Surprise me: cause analysis (js/app.js L2453-L2520)
- Mode odds in code: preset 30% / blend 20% / anti 18% / free 32% (observed 26/24/19/31 in 300 rolls).
- EVERY mode calls `randomizeProfileTypes()` (L2487), which sets `type_<section>` to a random category for 75% of all 22 sections (`rand() < 0.25 ? "" : random option`, L3507-L3515). The preset's own `profile` hints are therefore overwritten in the preset and blend modes (measured: forced share .794; hint realisation 19.3% of 596 hints vs 56.3% in a normal preset build). Only the anti mode restores hints, and only for `['values','beliefs','goals','origins','motivation','contradiction']` (L2488-L2494); because no preset hints goals, motivation or contradiction (section 1.5), 3 of those 6 restores are inert for every preset.
- It also sets divergence to 0.35-0.75 (`0.35 + round(rand*8)*0.05`, default is 0.15): measured divergence .6 -> coherence 4.0 (vs 4.9) and preset hint rate .29; divergence 1 -> .026.
- Net effect: "Surprised you from <preset>, pulled well off its defaults" is in practice a slider-jittered (+-45) sheet with random types: realised preset fidelity 64% (vs 71% default) and a 14.6 Strong+Jarring contradiction load in free mode (default 8.2).
- Fix: in preset/blend mode, randomise only sections the preset does NOT hint; keep divergence <= 0.3; in free mode multiply random slider magnitudes by ~0.6.
