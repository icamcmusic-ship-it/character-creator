# Project notes for Claude

## Never-allowed features

These are permanently out of scope. Do not implement them, and do not propose or list them in audits, reviews, roadmaps or feature suggestions.

- **Name generator.** Character names stay user-entered free text. Do not add name generation of any kind (random, setting-keyed, culture-keyed or lens-keyed).
- **Appearance generator and Appearance section.** The tool generates voice and personality only. Do not add, restore or propose an Appearance section, appearance traits, appearance sliders or appearance generation of any kind (build, upkeep, presence, movement and bearing, distinguishing marks, looks, clothing).
